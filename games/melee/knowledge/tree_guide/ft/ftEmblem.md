# ftEmblem — Roy (`ftFe_`; folder literally named "Fire Emblem")

Marth's clone, inverted: his sword is strongest at the **hilt**, weak at the
tip, and he falls faster. Mid-low tier ("we love Roy, Roy's our boy").
Only `ftFe_Init.c` is split; specials mirror [Marth's](ftMars.md):

- Flare Blade (neutral B) — chargeable fire slash; full charge is a one-hit
  KO that also hurts Roy
- Double-Edge Dance (side B) — fiery Dancing Blade
- Blazer (up B) — flaming Dolphin Slash, less height
- Counter (down B)
