# ftCrazyHand — Crazy Hand (`ftCh_`)

The erratic left hand that joins Classic-mode fights when conditions are met
(hard difficulty, fast clear). Structurally a mirror of
[Master Hand](ftMasterHand.md) — same file-per-gesture layout (`Drill`,
`Poke`, `Slam`, `Squeeze`, `FingerBeam`, `BackAirplane*`, `Tag*` co-op
attacks) plus his own tricks like the falling bombs
(`it/items/itcrazyhandbomb.c`). Shared plumbing in `ft/ftbosslib.c`.
