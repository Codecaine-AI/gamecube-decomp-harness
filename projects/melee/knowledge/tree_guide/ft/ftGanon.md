# ftGanon — Ganondorf (`ftGn_`)

Captain Falcon's clone, traded speed for bone-crushing power (in Melee he
fights with fists — no sword). Only `ftGn_Init.c` is split so far; his
specials mirror [Falcon's](ftCaptain.md):

- Warlock Punch (neutral B) — slower, stronger Falcon Punch
- Gerudo Dragon (side B) — dark Raptor Boost
- Dark Dive (up B) — electric grab-and-boom recovery
- Wizard's Foot (down B) — dark Falcon Kick (the aerial version's landing
  hitbox is a famous spike)
