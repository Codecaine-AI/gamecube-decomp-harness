# ftCLink — Young Link (`ftCl_`; "Child Link")

Link's clone, smaller and quicker with weaker kill power; fire arrows,
better mobility, wall-jump. Only two files split so far:

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `ftCl_AppealS.c` | His taunt — chugging Lon Lon Milk (`it/items/itclinkmilk.c`). |
| `ftCl_Init.c` | Spawn/load callbacks. |

Specials mirror [Link's](ftLink.md): Fire Bow / Boomerang / Spin Attack /
Bomb (his bombs explode on contact with anything).
