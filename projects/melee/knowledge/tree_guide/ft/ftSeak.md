# ftSeak — Sheik (`ftSk_`; romanized "Seak" internally)

Zelda's alter ego and a Melee top tier: fast, oppressive tilts/aerials,
brutal chaingrabs on fastfallers, needles ruling neutral.

| File | Move | Behavior |
|------|------|----------|
| `ftSk_SpecialN.c` | Needle Storm | Charge up to six needles; throws flat on the ground, angled-down in the air (`itseakneedleheld.c` / `thrown`). |
| `ftSk_SpecialS.c` | Chain | Whips a segmented chain around (`itseakchain.c`) — niche, mostly a ledge-stall toy. |
| `ftSk_SpecialHi.c` | Vanish | Explode-and-teleport recovery (`itseakvanish.c`). |
| `ftSk_SpecialLw.c` | Transform | Back to [Zelda](ftZelda.md). |
| `ftSk_Init.c` | — | Spawn/load callbacks. |
