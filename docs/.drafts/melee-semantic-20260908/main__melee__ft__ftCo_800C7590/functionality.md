## Animation, mash input, and release
The active motion-state table assigns the four owned callbacks to CaptureLeadead. Anim subtracts leadead_grab_timer_step, then calls ftCommon_GrabMash with leadead_grab_break_threshold, ignoring its boolean return. That second field is used as a decrement amount, despite its threshold spelling: the helper subtracts it once for any qualifying pressed-button mask and once more if either remembered stick direction changes. Stick comparisons are independent and strict against +/-x308. For nonnegative finite x308, equality at either boundary does not update that axis's remembered direction; for negative x308, the opposite comparison may still succeed, and where both succeed the later positive-direction assignment wins. It also updates mash/shake bookkeeping and reports the result to pl_800402D0. Only after this work does Anim test grab_timer<=0. At zero or below it calls it_802EAE80 on the retained item before ftCo_800C7800 on the fighter. No animation-completion test is involved. IASA and Phys are genuinely empty: no pointer dereference, calls, state changes, or velocity updates occur in those callbacks. This does not establish that the entire engine or item cannot move or interrupt the fighter. [code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftmotionstates.c#L3797-L3807; code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftCo_800C7590.c#L61-L74; code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L682-L719]

No timer clamping or finite-value validation is performed. Negative configured steps or mash amounts can increase the timer. Both signed zeros satisfy the completion comparison. A NaN at the final comparison does not satisfy <=0; positive infinity with finite decrements remains positive, and negative infinity satisfies the test unless preceding arithmetic produces NaN. These are source floating-comparison consequences, not claims that normal game data contains such values. Entry similarly does not validate coefficient ranges or the integer addition before conversion. Termination and monotonic decay depend on parameters and scheduling; finite positive decrements alone do not guarantee progress when floating-point rounding leaves the stored timer unchanged.

`ftCo_800C7800` writes -facing_dir*x370 to gr_vel only when ground_or_air==GA_Ground, otherwise to self_vel.x. It does not locally modify the other velocity component. Both branches then enter ftCo_MS_CaptureCut with flags 0, frame 0, rate 1 and zero/NULL trailing arguments, and write x73C to capture_timer after the state change. Opposite-facing recoil describes the ordinary positive-x370, normalized-facing case, not an invariant over arbitrary signs, zero, NaN or infinity.
