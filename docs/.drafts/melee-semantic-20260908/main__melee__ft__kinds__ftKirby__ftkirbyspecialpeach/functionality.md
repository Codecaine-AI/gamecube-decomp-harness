Ordinary animation consumes cmd_vars[1]==1 into 2, installs the attribute-backed ShieldDesc/counter callback, sets x221B_b3 and copies specialn_pe_unk3 into both shield fields. Zero clears x221B_b0; other values do not rearm. Animation completion independently dispatches grounded completion or Fall. Air physics interprets the command as unsigned. Zero selects basic fall; values >=1 select move-specific fall. Exactly 1 advances to 2 and either grants configured vertical momentum while setting xCC or zeros vertical velocity when xCC was already set. Air friction and collision follow-up run in every branch. Hit air physics directly applies move-specific falling/friction without that command gate or collision follow-up. Ordinary ground physics adds collision follow-up to common ground movement; Hit ground physics does not. Common air friction writes horizontal animation velocity, while fall modifies and clamps vertical self velocity.
