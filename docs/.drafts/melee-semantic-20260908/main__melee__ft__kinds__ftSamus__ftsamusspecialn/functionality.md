## Charge Shot lifecycle
The TU implements Samus's grounded and aerial neutral-special entry, startup, charging, cancellation, release, physics, and collision callbacks. Creation requires cmd_vars[0] == 1 and no retained article. It consumes the command before allocation, stores the resulting article, and returns true only on allocation failure; both startup callbacks ignore that result. Grounded startup tests the aerial-origin selector or exact equality with maximum charge before selecting literal destination 346 rather than 344. Aerial startup selects literal 348 after animation completion.

Holding consumes cmd_vars[2] for sound feedback and indexes a five-entry table using normalized charge, without a local bounds clamp. Its timer increments each update, and charge advances only when the timer is strictly greater than x20. Reaching the maximum clamps charge, starts color animation 53, and selects literal 345 without automatically firing. Hold IASA prioritizes the common escape check, then pressed B, then pressed LR—not held-button masks. Other IASA callbacks are empty.

Release requires cmd_vars[1] == 1 and a retained article, changes the command to 2, computes a planar launch position, passes stored and maximum charge to the item helper, and preserves item_gobj across the operation. Horizontal velocity is overwritten by facing_dir * x1C * stored_charge when motion_id == 348 or the fighter is airborne. Release then resets stored charge, clears the fighter-side article reference, removes guarded fighter effects, and spawns effect 1158. The detachment helper does not itself destroy the article or reset charge; item destruction calls it only for an unreleased Samus Charge article whose recorded origin still matches its owner. Interruption cleanup separately removes the article and effects and resets charge through the wrapper installed for take_dmg_cb and death2_cb.

## State boundaries and exceptional paths
Ground-loss callbacks use the common ground-to-air helper; hold uniquely plays SFX 260021 and arms the release command. Cancel ground loss does not arm release. Landing callbacks require the collision predicate to equal 1 and select literal destinations 343 or 346. Numeric destinations are preserved as source literals rather than normalized to enum identities. Grounded physics delegates friction and movement; aerial physics delegates gravity and aerial friction. The ground-friction multiplier's value is not established here, so stronger deceleration is not asserted. Grounded action completion normally reaches Wait but retains the common routine's exceptional branches. Aerial completion chooses ordinary Fall when x24 is zero, otherwise the FallSpecial helper with x24 as landing lag; that helper also has an exceptional early branch.

## Missile helpers
The trailing side-special accessory callback increments x2238 before attempting allocation, computes the launch position, selects the boolean variant using named motion predicates, requests effect 1155, and clears accessory4_cb even if allocation fails. Thus x2238 is launch-generation bookkeeping, not a live-missile count. Successful missile construction snapshots it. Subsequent item processing checks an unsigned generation difference of at least two when the reflection flag is clear and a recorded owner remains; reflection exempts the missile, and owner invalidation clears the recorded reference. The effect helper guards spawning with x2219_b0 but installs effect-hitlag callbacks on either path.
