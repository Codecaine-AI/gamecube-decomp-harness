## Physics and collision

Ordinary ground physics selects base friction or a strict-above-walk-speed multiplier. It writes acceleration and projects acceleration/ground velocity into ground-normal-aligned vectors, without directly changing gr_vel or integrating position. Multiplier magnitude is unverified. Dash physics adds x40C while frame<=x408 to x404-scaled character friction; the delegated helper can instead choose translationOffset.z*facing-gr_vel acceleration under x594_b0. Air physics calls fast-fall checking, fast-fall or gravity/terminal descent, then aerial control. 961D0 uses air physics for GA_Air and ground physics otherwise.
