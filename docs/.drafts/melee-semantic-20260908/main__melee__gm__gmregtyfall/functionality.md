## ToyFall presentation

This unit implements the opening fighter-and-trophy presentation, framebuffer overlays, and manual dismissal logic. It also supplies campaign-specific trophy lookup and a byte-result scene-exit helper used by the Congratulations scene. It does not locally demonstrate persistent trophy acquisition.

### Resource selection and staging

`gm_801A659C` maps Classic and Adventure game-over modes to their campaign kinds, delegates Debug selection to `gm_801BF050`, and uses All-Star for every other mode. Its source signature remains `bool(int)`, although callers consume its result as a trophy identifier; that spelling must not be interpreted as proof of boolean normalization. The delegated lookup selects among three character-indexed tables. `gm_801A6EE4` uses the same mode distinction to load the trophy model, three camera-cut scene roots, a background root, the stand scene, and a campaign-specific display archive. The final archive switch explicitly accepts both playable and game-over Classic/Adventure values. [Lookup](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L93-L105), [loader](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L363-L406), [delegated tables](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gm_1601.c#L508-L560).

`gm_801A68D8` initializes the gameplay-side environment and slot-0 demo fighter, using numeric camera mode 6 and creation mode 7. It sets the selected character/costume, facing +1, and initial position `(0,182,0)`, applies animation rate 0.6 to the primary and optional second entity, and normalizes scale through the character-indexed table. `fn_801A6868` ignores its GObj argument and moves slot 0 by approximately -0.19 Y per eligible invocation. Its inclusive `Y >= 125` guard permits a final step below the threshold; 300 is a nominal span divisor, not a guaranteed exact stopping count. [Setup and descent](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L180-L240).

Entry constructs lights, cameras, sprite layers, and main/trophy hierarchies. Trophy metadata supplies negative translations, negative degree-to-radian Y rotation, and a scale ratio; the character scale is applied separately to the root. Joint constraints connect the fighter and trophy presentation hierarchies. These paths assume valid resources and usable scale denominators rather than providing comprehensive failure recovery. [Helpers and entry](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L418-L571).

### Capture and overlay state

The capture camera writes two EFB representations into the selected `gm_804808F8` pair, while the SObj camera writes into `gm_804809D0`. Both paths require successful outer camera activation. The SObj delegate independently activates its camera; if that nested activation fails and returns, the outer callback still marks the capture ready and copies the EFB. Invalid render-pass kinds can panic inside camera activation. Thus failure must not be summarized as universally side-effect-free engine behavior. [Capture callbacks](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L242-L272), [nested delegate](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/sysdolphin/baselib/sobjlib.c#L543-L565), [activation](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/sysdolphin/baselib/cobj.c#L485-L525).

`fn_801A6664` always animates the main hierarchy. Once enabled, its first overlay is rebuilt each invocation, alternates image slots, and decreases its counter by six above 255 or three otherwise, clamping at zero and limiting displayed alpha to 255. The secondary overlay counts down from seven and rebuilds every eighth enabled invocation, alternates its selector, uses RGB `AA/AA/FF`, and raises alpha by ten up to 190. The divider's early return occurs after first-overlay processing. Capture-ready flags remain set until reinitialization; they are not per-frame acknowledgements. [Overlay process](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L117-L173).

The paired descriptors are initialized with dimensions 490×480 and numeric formats 5/0x16. The first copy does not clear or synchronize; the second clears and synchronizes. The standalone capture does not clear and does synchronize. The common camera renderer changes priority masks across three dispatch phases before capture. [Allocation](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L304-L361), [copy semantics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/sysdolphin/baselib/tobj.c#L1565-L1583), [renderer](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/cm/camera.c#L4586-L4616).

### Cross-file lifetimes and dismissal

`fn_801A6B6C` advances its camera and then tests exact equality of current and end frames. Completion tears down slot-0 entities, requests destruction of five stored GObjs, invokes `mn_8022F0F0(3)`, frees the four `gm_80480964` image pointers, constructs cut 2 through `gm_801A7B00`, and requests destruction of its own GObj. Generic GObj destruction has a deferred-current-object branch. This is a first-cut transition, not whole-scene exit. Notably, the explicitly freed bank is not `gm_804808F8`, and this unit does not establish complete cleanup of every capture allocation. [Completion](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L274-L297), [destruction](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/sysdolphin/baselib/gobjplink.c#L103-L127).

The successor reuses retained trophy/stand roots and `gm_804D67A8`; its camera endpoint progresses to `gm_801A9630`. Ending-display code subsequently consumes the retained display archive and cut-3 camera root, and uses trophy-record counts to select animation thresholds before requesting exit. Archive-backed resources therefore outlive the opening cut. [Cut 2](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gm_1A7A.c#L35-L154), [ending display](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregenddisp.c#L280-L442).

Entry initializes a 30-update input lockout. `gm_Scene_ToyFall_OnFrame` ignores Start while decrementing it, including the invocation that reaches zero. Later newly triggered Start performs SFX key-off, forward feedback, and a runner state-1 exit request. Separately, `gm_801A6630` writes the low byte of its argument to current-scene exit data before requesting exit; the Congratulations callback supplies 6 for Debug and 1 otherwise after its closing countdown. [Dismissal](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L573-L582), [exit helper](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gmregtyfall.c#L107-L112), [Congratulations caller](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gm/gm_1A9B.c#L143-L166).
