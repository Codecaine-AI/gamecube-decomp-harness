## ThrownKoopa victim-state support

This unit defines two setup routines and sixteen empty Anim/IASA/Phys/Coll callbacks for ThrownKoopaF, ThrownKoopaB, ThrownKoopaAirF and ThrownKoopaAirB. Its header declares all eighteen functions. Empty callbacks establish only the absence of local phase processing, not the absence of common-engine or captor processing. [Source](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_ThrownKoopa.c#L14-L74), [header](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_ThrownKoopa.h#L6-L23).

### Initial entry

`ftCo_800BCDE0` passes the requested motion, `Ft_MF_None` and frame zero to `ftCo_Thrown_Enter`, then calls `ftAnim_8006EBA4`. The shared inline copies linked-fighter facing, clears `capturekoopa.x0`, changes motion with the linked fighter as animation source, installs `ftCo_800DE508`, and applies mask `0x1FF`. It does not guard the linked object. The animation helper invokes an immediate animation/action-processing sequence. [Wrapper](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_ThrownKoopa.c#L14-L19), [inline](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/inlines.h#L28-L38), [animation helper](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftanim.c#L380-L386).

Captor Hit and Wait IASA paths independently establish the victim motion domain. The shared directional routine selects forward when `specials.facing_dir == +1`, otherwise backward, using the grounded or aerial ThrownKoopa pair supplied by its caller. `ftCo_ThrownKoopa_Enter` is therefore a supported descriptive alias, not a recovered historical spelling or evidence derived from rendering. [Callers](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftKoopa/ftkoopaspecials.c#L479-L572).

### Frame-preserving continuation

The active `SOLUTION == 1` body of `ftCo_800BCE64` copies linked-fighter facing, clears `capturekoopa.x0`, and changes to the requested motion at the victim's current frame, with rate 1, blend 0 and the linked fighter as animation source. It reinstalls the accessory callback and applies `0x1FF`. Flags are SkipMatAnim, SkipColAnim, UpdateCmd, SkipItemVis, Unk19, SkipModelPartVis, SkipModelFlags and Unk27. The disabled shared-inline alternative is a compile-time alternative, not a runtime branch. Unknown flag meanings remain unspecified. [Implementation](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_ThrownKoopa.c#L21-L42).

Air-ending collision callbacks route to captor continuations that perform ground conversion, change the captor to literal state 351 or 352 at its current frame, conditionally reverse facing before and after the change, and invoke this helper only when a victim exists. Victim destinations are explicitly ThrownKoopaF and ThrownKoopaB. The victim helper itself neither tests landing nor performs ground conversion. The descriptive alias `ftCo_ThrownKoopa_AirToGround` is supported by caller context; exact symbolic identities of captor literals 351/352 remain deferred. [Continuations](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftKoopa/ftkoopaspecials.c#L249-L297), [routing](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftKoopa/ftkoopaspecials.c#L668-L676).

### Cross-file lifetime and exceptional paths

`ftCo_800DE508` derives victim position from its XRotN joint, facing, scale and offsets, forcing position Z to zero; it does not directly read the captor object. Captor forward-ending animation performs command-triggered release/launch externally and checks victim existence. A separate ground-ending collision continuation releases an existing victim and enters Fall for that victim, then enters Fall for the captor. These are not behaviors of the sixteen empty victim callbacks. Exact accessory teardown and linked-object lifetime remain unestablished.
