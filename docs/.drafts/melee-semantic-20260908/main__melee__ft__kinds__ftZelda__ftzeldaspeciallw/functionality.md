## Transform startup and deferred handoff
Grounded and aerial entries select states 355 and 357, initialize animation processing, and run the shared startup helper. It clears `cmd_vars[0]`, divides horizontal self velocity and ground velocity by `x70`, divides vertical self velocity by `x74`, obtains a TopN-derived position, calls `lb_800119DC` with 120, 0.4, 0.003 and 60 degrees converted to radians, and installs `ftZd_SpecialLw_8013ADB4`. Division is established; attenuation depends on unverified attribute values. [Source](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftZelda/ftzeldaspeciallw.c#L73-L117)

The startup effect callback conditionally spawns effect 1276 on `parts[104].joint` when `x2219_b0` is clear, then sets the latch. Effect-hitlag setup and clearing `accessory4_cb` occur regardless of spawning. Both startup animation callbacks publish `ftZd_SpecialLw_8013AEAC` into that slot on animation exhaustion rather than calling it immediately. The deferred callback clears the slot before passing Sheik's `ftSk_SpecialLw_80114758` to `ftCommon_8007EFC8`; the supplied initializer selects Sheik finish state 0x16A or 0x16C. ## Destination-side Zelda finishing

Sheik's deferred callback supplies `ftZd_SpecialLw_8013B4D8`. That initializer chooses 356 when `ground_or_air == 0`, otherwise 358, starts at `x80`, and installs `ftZd_SpecialLw_8013AE30`. ## Physics, collision and callback lifetimes
All four IASA callbacks are no-ops, which does not imply global immunity to interruption. Grounded physics delegates to `ft_80084F3C`, applying ground friction with conditional scaling above walking speed and then ground movement. Aerial physics passes `x78` and `x7C` to `ftCommon_Fall`, then calls `ftCommon_8007CEF4`, without a local transition. ## Din's Fire services
Despite their canonical SpecialLw prefix, the final four helpers serve the side-special projectile lifecycle. `8013B540` is read-only and returns exactly 1 for motion 344 or 347 with nonnull `x222C`, otherwise 0. Its item consumer also requires saved-origin/current-owner equality and reflection-related permission before sampling input and changing heading, speed and planar velocity. `8013B574` succeeds only with nonnull `x222C`, motion 345 or 348, and `cmd_vars[1] == 1`; only success consumes that command. The signal initiates state-1 presentation/lifetime behavior, not immediate creation of the separate explosion. `8013B5C4` only clears the fighter's tracked projectile and secondary-death/damage callbacks. Item destruction and terminal explosion handoff call it after saved-owner validation. `8013B5EC` instead calls `it_802C3D44` before clearing the fighter handle; that item routine clears ownership, the retained Zelda reference and an ownership-related flag without destroying the item. Successful creation registers `ftZd_Init_801393AC` in both event slots, and that wrapper forwards to detachment. Both cleanup helpers clear callbacks even with no tracked projectile. Preserve terminal clear-only cleanup, interruption-time detachment and item destruction as distinct operations.
