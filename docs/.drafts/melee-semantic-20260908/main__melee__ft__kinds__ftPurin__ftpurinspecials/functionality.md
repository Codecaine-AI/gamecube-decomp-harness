## Side-special lifecycle
The unit implements Purin/Jigglypuff's grounded and airborne side-special callbacks, associated with Pound in the frozen baseline. Ground entry selects numeric motion state `0x16B`; aerial entry selects `0x16C`. Both call `ftAnim_8006EBA4` after changing motion state and then clear `cmd_vars[0..3]`. Neither entry locally resets `u.pr.x222C`. [Entries](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPurin/ftpurinspecials.c#L35-L52).

Animation callbacks do nothing locally while frames remain. Ground animation exhaustion delegates to `ft_8008A2BC`; aerial exhaustion calls `ftCo_Fall_Enter`. Both IASA callbacks are empty: this establishes no callback-local cancellation processing, not a global prohibition on interruption. Inherited contextual research establishes that the grounded completion dispatcher has hand-boss branches and, on its ordinary path, checks DownSpot and another exceptional condition before Wait, with additional common processing afterward. It is not an unconditional Wait transition. [Callbacks](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPurin/ftpurinspecials.c#L54-L75); [dispatcher](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_08A1.c#L53-L109).

## Movement
Ground physics delegates entirely to `ft_80084FA8`. Inherited contextual research describes friction selection according to ground speed versus maximum walking speed, followed by animation-derived acceleration or friction and ground movement. [Shared physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_084E.c#L55-L89).

Aerial physics consumes nonzero `cmd_vars[0]` by clearing it, incrementing `u.pr.x222C`, and replacing horizontal and vertical self-velocity. The angle calculation applies `stickGetDir`, caps against attribute `xE0`, subtracts `xDC`, floors at zero, restores negative-input sign, and converts `value * xE4 / (xE0 - xDC)` to radians. There is no local denominator guard. Speed comes from `xF0`; facing direction affects the horizontal cosine component. Independently of whether launch occurred, `cmd_vars[1]` selects 0 → `ft_80084EEC`, 1 → multiply both velocity components by `xF4`, or 2 → `ft_80084DB0`. Other values perform no switch action but do not undo an already processed launch. [Angle and physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPurin/ftpurinspecials.c#L77-L126).

## Collision continuity and shared side effects
Ground collision calls the aerial conversion helper exactly when `ft_800827A0(gobj) == GA_Ground`; aerial collision calls the grounded conversion helper exactly when `ft_80081D0C(gobj) != GA_Ground`. Enum spelling and rendered `ft_CheckGround` must not substitute for these comparisons. Inherited contextual research establishes that the former query returns a boolean map-collision result; the latter updates collision positions and returns `GA_Ground` on its `ft_80081A00` exceptional path, otherwise selecting its result from the map query. [Local conditions](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPurin/ftpurinspecials.c#L128-L155); [ground query](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_081B.c#L406-L424); [aerial query](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_081B.c#L105-L123).

Shared inline implementations reuse `cur_anim_frame` rather than invoking fresh-entry callbacks. The air-to-ground helper subsequently clamps horizontal self-velocity using the normal air-drift maximum. Inherited shared-situation analysis also establishes jump/ECB changes and ground support validation with an assertion path. Continuity therefore means retained animation progress under supplied flags, not preservation of every fighter field.
