## Nana partner-side SpecialS

This unit implements Nana's participation in the paired Ice Climbers side special, conventionally mapped to Squall Hammer. It contains a collision box, join and cleanup helpers, two motion initializers, and grounded/aerial animation, IASA, physics and collision callback families. Canonical source—not substituted rendered names—supports these roles.

### Admission and initialization
`ftNn_Init_80123954` returns true for a null Nana, excluded numeric statuses 1–13, or failure of the proximity test. For a present rejected Nana it clears her `x1A5C`; it does not perform reciprocal teardown. Admission requires `dx*dx + dy*dy < (s32)(popo_scale_y * (xD0*xD0))`: the strict comparison, integer cast and single scale factor are significant. Popo is resolved through Nana's player slot at entity index 0 without a local null guard. Successful admission returns false, selects motion 360 for `GA_Air` or motion 359 otherwise, resets the spacing counter, and copies Popo's position, self velocity, ground velocity and facing into Nana before storing Popo's object in `x1A5C`. Popo's callers subsequently create the reciprocal pointer and choose their paired rather than solo states. [Nana admission](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftNana/ftnanaspecials.c#L42-L108); [Popo callers](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecials.c#L121-L201).

Both initializers clear four command variables, install damage/death and effect-hitlag callbacks before and after changing motion, call `ftAnim_8006EBA4`, and zero vertical animation/self velocity. These are entry-local resets: the coordinator subsequently replaces self velocity with Popo's. The conventional inferred entry names remain hypotheses. [Initializers](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftNana/ftnanaspecials.c#L122-L154).

### Continued participation and termination
The animation callbacks copy Popo's current animation frame while his paired-motion predicate returns false. Otherwise they perform reciprocal unlink/reset and clear Nana's damage, secondary-death and hitlag callbacks. The grounded variant calls `ft_8008A2BC`; the aerial variant enters ordinary Fall when `x12C == 0`, otherwise calls `ftCommon_8007D60C` followed by `ftCo_80096900(nana, 1, 0, 1, 1.0f, x12C)`. Nana's own predicate returns false only for numeric motions 359 and 360; Popo's paired animation callbacks use it alongside animation exhaustion to terminate their participation. [Nana callbacks](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftNana/ftnanaspecials.c#L110-L199); [Popo predicate](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecials.c#L95-L103); [Popo termination](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecials.c#L242-L301).

`ftNn_Init_801238E4` resets part-0 X rotation, calls `Fighter_UnkSetFlag_8006CFBC` on Nana and an optional linked fighter, and clears both references. Its inferred OnDamage name must not imply damage-exclusive use: normal animation completion invokes it too. The registered damage/death wrapper in `ftnana.c` also forwards to it. [Cleanup](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftNana/ftnanaspecials.c#L29-L40); [Wrapper](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftNana/ftnana.c#L379-L382).

### Physics and terrain
Both IASA callbacks immediately return. Both physics callbacks unconditionally copy Popo's self velocity, animation velocity, ground velocity, ground acceleration and facing, then set part-0 Y rotation to `M_PI_2 * facing_dir`. They do not copy position or initiate transitions. [IASA and physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftNana/ftnanaspecials.c#L201-L237).

Collision processing directly follows Popo only when both Popo predicates are false. It copies position, invokes collision-data transfer, changes motion on a ground/air mismatch, and then copies Popo's ground/air field. The mismatch targets are literally 360 in `_0_Coll` and 359 in `_1_Coll`; they are not dynamically derived from Popo's field. Otherwise the spacing counter increments and caps at five, and Nana's X becomes `Popo.x - 1.5 * counter * facing`. This increases the trailing offset rather than necessarily moving Nana toward Popo. The grounded fallback transitions on a false `ft_80082888` result; the aerial fallback transitions on a true `ft_800824A0` result. Those fallback transition helpers reset commands and perform ground/air conversion; landing also clears vertical self velocity. Both preserve the current animation-frame argument and pass mask `0x0C4C528A`. Every collision update then copies Popo's part-0 X rotation and reinstalls callbacks. [Collision implementation](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftNana/ftnanaspecials.c#L239-L367).

The box is declared as `ftCollisionBox ftNn_Unk2_803CDD60 = {12, 0, {-6, 6}, {6, 6}}`. Source establishes its initializer and consumers, but does not establish compiled section extent, constant-pool membership, alignment or emitted scalar encodings. Those baseline claims remain unresolved.
