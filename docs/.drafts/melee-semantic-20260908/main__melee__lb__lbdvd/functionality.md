# DVD Preload Cache

Maintains a DVD preload table with registration scores, one selected asynchronous read at a time through the scheduler, lazy type-specific postprocessing, mutating readiness polls and requested/committed scene reconciliation. Lifecycle state and load_state are separate; persistent_heap 6 is the scheduling-idle sentinel, distinct from persistent_heaps policy level.

Requested scene is scene; new_scene is the committed copy. Reconciliation compares and copies before interrupt masking, then negates old positive scores and re-registers required resources. State-1 stale entries are freed, state-4 stale entries become 3. Readiness polling can promote 3 to 4 with score 9999. Polling and retrieval are therefore mutating operations.

Registration matches entry number plus the matching-heap argument, not all resource metadata. Pending archive lookup assigns 8980 even if the old score was larger. The scheduler selects the first greatest positive score and suppresses work while any state 2 exists. Type 0 allocates but performs no DVD read; archive allocation is not independently checked. Initialization overwrites prior handles without cleanup.
