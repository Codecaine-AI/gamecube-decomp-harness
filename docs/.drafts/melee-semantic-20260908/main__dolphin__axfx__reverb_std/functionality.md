## Standard AX reverb

The unit constructs, processes and destroys persistent standard-reverb state. Each of three channel-state groups has two parallel comb delays and two all-pass delays. The fixed sample lags are 0x6FD, 0x7CF, 0x1B1 and 0x95; buffers reserve two additional samples. Comb feedback is derived from decay time at 32000 samples/second. Coloration becomes the all-pass coefficient, mix becomes the output level, and damping is clamped then remapped. [Construction](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/extern/dolphin/src/dolphin/axfx/reverb_std.c#L20-L100).

HandleReverb processes three consecutive 160-sample banks in place. Its pipeline is optional predelay, parallel combs, first all-pass, damped history, second all-pass, then dry/wet mixing. Wet weight is 0.6*level and dry weight is 0.6*(1-level); 0.3 scales the input to the damping recurrence. Integer conversion uses the explicit double bias and output truncation instructions. Filter indices, last outputs, damping history and predelay cursors persist between callbacks. The private callback passes only left to the processor: right and surround are not independently dereferenced. [DSP and dispatch](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/extern/dolphin/src/dolphin/axfx/reverb_std.c#L135-L408).

Initialization, settings replacement and shutdown bracket their work with saved interrupt masking. Initialization clears the callback gate even when creation returns failure. Settings ignores modification failure and always returns 1. Modification accepts predelay through 100.0 before freeing existing buffers, whereas creation rejects values above 0.1; therefore values in (0.1,100] can leave freed state exposed after settings clears the gate. Shutdown does not clear stored pointers or disable the callback. The audio driver unregisters the old bus callback before shutdown and selects a replacement callback only after successful initialization. [Lifecycle](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/extern/dolphin/src/dolphin/axfx/reverb_std.c#L102-L133), [public wrappers](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/extern/dolphin/src/dolphin/axfx/reverb_std.c#L410-L470), [driver lifetime](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/sysdolphin/baselib/axdriver.c#L949-L1020).

Predelay allocation tests the floating setting, while processing and freeing test the converted sample count. Very small positive settings can therefore request zero-byte buffers without activating or freeing them through the predelay branches. The assembly wraps an advanced cursor on equality with base+4*(count-1), not the allocation's one-past-end address; count one does not wrap after its first increment. Allocation results are not checked before memset, and range comparisons do not explicitly reject NaNs. [Allocation](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/extern/dolphin/src/dolphin/axfx/reverb_std.c#L28-L99), [cursor handling](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/extern/dolphin/src/dolphin/axfx/reverb_std.c#L175-L222).
