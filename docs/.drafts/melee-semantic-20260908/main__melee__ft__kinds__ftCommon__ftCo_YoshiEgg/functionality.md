# ftCo_YoshiEgg semantic review

## Scope, verification and naming
The complete owned C file and header were independently read in canonical and rendered form, and all 24 subjects, 65 facts and 22 outgoing links were enumerated. The librarian's analysis is substantially corroborated, particularly its corrections to countdown boundaries, callback lifetime and unsupported section/layout claims. This review additionally does not retain the module data-flow claim that accessory scaling is copied from Yoshi: the initial accessory scale comes from the victim's common attributes. Statements promising an unconditional Fall destination are also not retained as proved.

This TU implements the captive fighter side of Yoshi SpecialN/Egg Lay, not the attacking fighter's animation or the separate egg item. The active YoshiEgg motion-table entry installs Anim, IASA, Phys and Coll together with ftCamera_UpdateCameraBox; Yoshi's grounded and aerial SpecialN victim paths call the preparation and entry routines. These are active table/caller observations, not deductions from numeric enum labels. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftmotionstates.c#L3181-L3191 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispecialn.c#L405-L447.

Canonical address-named functions remain authoritative. Rendered ftCo_YoshiEgg_Scale, _Enter, _Ground, _Air and _OnHit2 describe plausible roles for ftCo_800BBCC0, ftCo_800BBED4, ftCo_800BC388, ftCo_800BC3AC and ftCo_800BC3D0, respectively; they are not recovered original names. External rendered names likewise do not prove meaning. In particular, rendered GetEggJObj obscures the actual HSD_Joint* descriptor returned by ftYs_SpecialN_8012CDD4 from source ft_data->x48_items[3]. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispecialn.c#L122-L127.

## Preparation and entry
ftCo_800BBC88 resolves the supplied Fighter, sets invisible=true and calls ftColl_8007B62C(gobj,2), without a local guard or motion-state change. The inspected caller requires cmd_vars[1] and a non-null capture victim, then clears that command. A separate cmd_vars[0] branch obtains victim_gobj and calls ftCo_800BBED4(victim_gobj,gobj). Both branches can execute in one update; the earlier preparation is not guaranteed to occur a frame earlier, nor does the entry branch require cmd_vars[1] to have been active. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L33-L38 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispecialn.c#L405-L432.

ftCo_800BBED4 takes the victim first and the source/capturing fighter second. It applies ftCommon_8007D5D4 only if initially grounded, enters ftCo_MS_YoshiEgg with Ft_MF_Unk06 and the source object, installs ftCo_800BC438 as take_dmg_cb, calls ftCommon_8007E2F4(fp,0x1FF), and hides the victim. It loads an accessory from the source descriptor, scales it uniformly using victim co_attrs.xBC.size, saves that scale by value, and links its child to the mapped victim TransN joint. inlineA0 calls ftColl_8007B0C0 with HurtCapsule_Intangible and initializes the first hurt capsule using mapped TransN, HurtHeight_Mid, is_grabbable=false, and victim xBC offsets and scale. Entry initializes velocity, facing and dmg.x182c_behavior from source accessors, retains arg1 in yoshiegg.x0, installs ftCo_800BC3D0 as take_dmg_2_cb, calls ftCommon_8007EFC0(fp,1), initializes interpolation inputs and grab state, saves the root scale, and installs accessory4_cb. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L71-L129.

The source velocity is (-source facing*dat_attrs.x10,dat_attrs.x14,0); source dat_attrs.x18 supplies damage behavior, x20 the interpolation denominator, x1C its factor and x24 initial grab duration. Recurring timer decay, mash decrement, mash-animation duration/rate, release configuration/velocity and damage coefficient instead use global Yoshi ext_attr. The damage coefficient is ext_attr.x44/ext_attr.x18 without a zero-denominator guard. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispecialn.c#L39-L120.

Canonical initialization writes mv.ca.specialhi.vel.y=0 and yoshiegg.x14=mv.co.walk.fast_anim_frame. This review does not rewrite them as yoshiegg.x8=0 or x14=x10: those union-layout equivalences lack compiled evidence. Source spellings, not inferred offsets, define the supported description.

## Scale callback and numeric boundaries
ftCo_800BBCC0 first tests yoshiegg.x14<=0. On that path it calls Fighter_UpdateModelScale, clears accessory4_cb and returns, without a local accessory-scale reset. Otherwise it subtracts one from x14, computes m=(1-xC)+((x10-x14)/x10)*xC, multiplies saved root x18 and accessory scale vectors componentwise by m, and writes both JObj scales. A decrement reaching exactly zero still performs the scale writes; cleanup requires another invocation. Fractional countdowns may overshoot. Zero/negative denominators, negative scale components or unrestricted factors invalidate unconditional growth claims. Under ordinary nontrapping floating-point comparison semantics NaN x14 bypasses cleanup, positive infinity does not count down normally, and negative infinity takes cleanup. Large finite values can also fail to change under subtraction by one. Target exception configuration and reachability of such values remain unresolved. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L40-L69.

## Captivity update and release
Anim subtracts global x28, calls ftCommon_GrabMash with global x2C and stores its boolean result in yoshiegg.x4, then tests grab_timer<=0. GrabMash subtracts once for eligible pressed buttons and independently once for a change in remembered stick direction, so both decrements can occur together. Stick comparisons are strict against the configured threshold; returning to neutral does not itself reset the remembered direction. InitGrab(...,false,...) disables the optional shake-count branch. GrabMash still updates bookkeeping and reports the result through pl_800402D0. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L670-L719.

On expiration Anim plays SFX 280088 with 127/64, queues effect 1231 with argument 4, the TopN joint and a pointer to victim co_attrs.xBC, overwrites self_vel from global release tuning, applies airborne setup, updates model scale, calls ftColl_8007B760 with the boolean release attribute, invokes ftCo_Fall_Enter_YoshiEgg and returns. Sound/effect IDs and exact collision-flag semantics remain unmapped here. The Fall helper first allows x2224_b2 to route to ftCo_80090780, then allows ftCo_800C5240 to route to ftCo_800C5D34; only its default route explicitly enters ftCo_MS_Fall with Ft_MF_Unk06 and blend argument -1. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L131-L165 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Fall.c#L31-L83.

On the non-expired path any nonzero x8 is decremented, including negative values. If the resulting x8<=0 and x4 is false, animation rate becomes 1 and x8 becomes zero. Independently, x8<=0 with true x4 reloads global x30 and sets animation rate from global x34. Initially zero x8 with no mash does nothing. There is no finite-value validation or general timer saturation: NaN grab_timer fails the release test, and NaN x8 enters the nonzero branch but fails the nonpositive comparisons. Positive tuning is necessary for the ordinary decay/escape interpretation; eventual expiration is not proved for arbitrary parameters.

IASA is an empty void(Fighter_GObj*) callback with no parameter dereference, calls or transitions. Dedicated Anim logic processes escape input. The empty IASA does not establish immunity from external forced state changes, damage or death. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L131-L167.

## Damage, physics and collision
ftCo_800BC3D0 evaluates grab_timer=-(dmg.x1838_percentTemp*coefficient-grab_timer), conditionally overwrites it with zero when dmg.x18CC==3 and ftCo_800C0C88(dmg.x18D0) succeeds, then writes x1828=4. Algebraic subtraction is an explanatory equivalence, not a bit-exact replacement for all exceptional floating-point inputs. Positive finite damage and coefficient ordinarily reduce duration; negative/nonfinite values need not. The predicate accepts BuryType_Unk1 and BuryType_Unk3 and rejects BuryType_Unk2/default. No gameplay identity is inferred from these unknown classifications. The damage callback does not transition states; Anim modifies the timer again before checking it. The primary callback ftCo_800BC438 only calls Fighter_UpdateModelScale. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L197-L206, code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Bury.c#L209-L219 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_CaptureKoopa.c#L20-L23.

Phys forwards the unchanged object to ft_80084F3C for GA_Ground and ft_80084DB0 otherwise. Coll sends ground to ft_8008403C(gobj,ftCo_800BC3AC) and every other value to ft_80082C74(gobj,ftCo_800BC388). The wrappers synchronously invoke those callbacks when ft_80082708 is false or ft_80081D0C is true, respectively; they do not retain them. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L169-L195, code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_081B.c#L1035-L1041 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_081B.c#L549-L556.

ftCo_800BC3AC wraps ftCommon_8007D5D4, setting GA_Air, zero ground velocity, attack/shield-knockback Z, position Z and animation Y velocity, one used jump, ECB lock 10 and the locked collision flag. ftCo_800BC388 wraps ftCommon_8007D7FC: conditional prior-air bookkeeping precedes grounding, optional translated-motion horizontal velocity, clamping/transfer to gr_vel, jump and wall-jump reset, ECB unlock, and supporting-floor validation that can assert. Neither local adapter changes motion state. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L509-L594.

## Ownership, dependencies and conventions
Except for empty IASA, the routines require valid fighter objects and expected model/attribute resources. Entry has no local null checks for either object or accessory-load failure. SetAccessory asserts on an already-present accessory and creates a JObj through HSD_JObjLoadJoint; this is not a transfer of a live source JObj. Scales are copied Vec3 values. The retained source pointer has no local ownership acquisition or subsequent local dereference. Full source lifetime, accessory teardown and queued effect argument lifetime remain unresolved. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L977-L984 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L91-L165.

The accessory callback is scheduler-gated, not guaranteed once per wall-clock frame. Fighter_8006C80C calls it only when !x221F_b3 and !x2219_b5, after effect-queue flushing and transformations. Motion-state callback setup clears accessory4_cb and both damage slots; entry installs them after its transition, and scale completion can independently unregister itself. This does not prove every exceptional exit's cleanup. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/fighter.c#L1370-L1389 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/fighter.c#L2552-L2572.

Dependencies include common fighter state/grab/collision/animation services, Yoshi attribute accessors, Fall/Bury/CaptureKoopa helpers, asynchronous effects, bone mapping, lb attachment operations and HSD JObj transforms. The header declares nine public void functions and excludes the static scale helper and inline hurtbox helper. Its older-looking address comments and include guard do not prove current addresses. PAD_STACK and FORCE_PAD_STACK are decompilation stack-shaping conventions, not gameplay. No authored global object appears in the owned source. All five .sdata/.sdata2 claims remain unresolved: source literals and inline helper usage cannot prove compiled section size, contents, mutability or placement. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.h#L1-L17 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_YoshiEgg.c#L1-L207.
