## Functionality

- **Hand encounter controller:** Initializes persistent encounter state and a GObj process, prepares Crazy Hand resources, and manages states 0–7. Master Hand begins alone; Crazy Hand's staged arrival requires both half-HP eligibility and an additional predicate. The spawn request can fail downstream, although the controller advances its arrival substep. Saved fighter modes are restored after entry. Individual and final defeats have distinct timed camera, slow-motion, sound, flash, and termination paths.
- **Campaign construction and results:** Shared helpers initialize campaign storage, build Game Over payloads, process continue/exit decisions, record campaign clears, and construct matches from callback-bearing campaign data. Match construction preserves special-event reuse, character substitutions, teammate packing, reserved and disabled slots, boss rules, and ordered numeric classification overrides. Stage 0x49 overrides even the boss classification. Result processing has debug-gated cancellation, trophy commitment, stock exhaustion, timeout decrement/replay, and flag-0x40/0x80 exceptions; its Boolean result permits progression rather than simply reporting victory.
- **Adventure preparation:** The encounter preload helper dispatches on the live match's stage kind, loads the selected fighter resources and Kirby Copy Ability resources, and sets camera quake scale to 0.2.
- **Allied rosters:** Random selection shuffles a shared candidate pool and excludes locked characters, four used-character entries, and three preset opponents. Exhaustion returns CHKIND_NONE. Generation still populates attributes and returns the requested count in that case. The teammate constructor packs populated descriptors after player zero, inherits that player's team, preserves the Giga Bowser exception, and ignores its third argument. The descriptor-reset helper clears teammates, not opponents.
- **Campaign queries:** Compact indices are 0 Adventure, 1 Classic, and 2 All-Star, distinct from the GameMode enum. Unsupported selectors return NULL or -1; the current-mode index query returns 3 outside the supported trio. Canonical gm_GetAllStarData supplies Classic's aggregate here, as independently confirmed by Classic's loader and consumers.
- **Deferred trophies:** Match setup invalidates twelve staged identifiers without clearing quantity bytes. Item and Figure Get producers update or insert quantities; the total query sums active entries. Commitment forwards active pairs to Toy_SetUnlockState without clearing them. Toy may update mode-local trophy staging rather than permanent storage directly, and repeated commitment resubmits quantities. Local quantities wrap as bytes, unlike Toy's saturating acquisition updates; signed result and identifier conversions require care.
