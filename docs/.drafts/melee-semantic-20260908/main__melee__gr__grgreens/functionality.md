## Green Greens stage controller
The module registers Gr_Kind_Greens and /GrGr.dat with seven Ground callback rows. Init caches yakumono parameters, sets stage flags, and requests objects 0, 2, 6, 5, 4. The factory installs rendering and optional initialization, process and callback3 handlers; acquisition failure reports and returns NULL. Object 6 creates initial block objects indirectly, including row-1 Star Blocks and row-3 Bomb Blocks. Its initializer clears the demo latch before objects 5 and 4 are created, then raises local/global startup gates. Ground retains the queued GObj/callback pair until after on_start, invokes it and frees the queue node.

Whispy uses a ten-entry repeating schedule of idle (0), wind (1) and apple (2) modes with separate entry/substate fields. It clears active wind before either startup guard. The fighter callback applies signed horizontal velocity only inside strict, endpoint-order-independent rectangles, with zero Y/Z and a fully zero fallback; a configured speed of zero does not change the applicability boolean. Wind direction is seeded from a helper result compared to +1.0, and wind substates use distinct animation flag predicates. Active wind is published as direction+1 only in substate 4. Threshold tests are strict and counters are animation-query gated. Apple spawning is subordinate to the animation-completion branch, uses x20 >= 0, alternates sides, randomizes position, and passes x74/x78 to the item constructor. Downstream apple outcomes are not implemented in this file.

The block controller owns a 5-row by 6-column position lattice and block-record grid. Thirty required model joints yield separate negative/nonnegative-X bounds; each side becomes a 5x3 lattice. Layout generation clears 30 entries and selects 18 fixed occupied cells; x20 controls type selection, whereas x8..x1C supply height-dependent column weights narrowed to u8. A zero weight excludes its entire three-column half. Both halves excluded means no spawn, but the countdown still resets. Spawning requires an empty destination and unused resource index, maps kind 1 to row 1 and kind 2 to row 3, and starts status 1 at the upper blast boundary or other statuses at grid height. It does not clear x1_6.

Item proxies retain the Ground owner and model JObj; separate damage-dealt and touch hooks are no-ops, while damage received resolves the first active matching item and asserts on failure. Destruction is one-shot via x1_7. Ordinary blocks mark cleanup immediately; bomb blocks hide their model and wait for the item command pointer to clear before cleanup. Collision notification sets x1_4 on the first nonempty matching joint. Only status-1 movement consumes that flag to initiate destruction, rather than treating it as fall initialization. Otherwise velocity accelerates with a cap, status 2 follows a lower moving block, and strict height crossing settles status 3 and contiguous upper status-2 cells. Cleanup destroys resources, marks the record empty, sets x1_6, compacts complete records and restarts scanning. Collision refresh occurs before pending adjacency work, where x1_5 takes priority over x1_6. Neighbor repair preserves the exceptional final branch: it tests col-1 but passes col+1, with a possible negative-index read at col 0.

Most existing names and explanations fit and are explicitly retained. Supported changes concern demo-latch lifetime, nested row-1 creation, collision-triggered movement, retained x1_6, asymmetric neighbor repair, and a more specific BombBlock initializer name. No compiled section placement or extent is established. The cleanup callback frees corneria union members; equivalence to the greens allocations remains unverified. Rendered hypotheses were checked against canonical code: the header suppresses grGreens_80216E64 as shadowed_binding, and two distinct animation predicates collide on grAnime_IsAnimFinished. These renderer issues are not evidence of canonical behavior.
