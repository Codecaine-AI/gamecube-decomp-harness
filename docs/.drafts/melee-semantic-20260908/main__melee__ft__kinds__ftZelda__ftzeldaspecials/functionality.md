### Loop exits and timing
Loop Anim decrements x0 and x4 whenever nonzero. With no tracked item, both must be nonpositive before requesting state 345/348. With a tracked item, it checks the actual item owner and clears tracking on mismatch. These branches are mutually exclusive: ownership-loss clearing does not also perform the no-item exit in that invocation. Nonzero decrement is not general saturation for negative inputs. [Ground loop](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftZelda/ftzeldaspecials.c#L133-L189), [air loop](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftZelda/ftzeldaspecials.c#L261-L315).

Loop IASA provides an independent exit: decrement xC, clamp nonpositive results to zero, and, once that gate expires, request state 345/348 when held_buttons[0] lacks mask 512. It does not require item absence or expired x0/x4. Startup and End IASA callbacks are empty; that establishes no IASA processing, not immunity to collision, damage, or other engine transitions. [IASA family](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftZelda/ftzeldaspecials.c#L350-L407).

### Cross-file lifetime and evidence limits
The installed damage/death callback forwards to ftZd_SpecialLw_8013B5EC. That path detaches the item through it_802C3D44, then clears fighter tracking and callbacks.
