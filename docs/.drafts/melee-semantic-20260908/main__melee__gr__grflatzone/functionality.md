### Registration and startup

The module exports `Gr_Kind_Flatzone`, `/GrFz.dat`, and nine `StageCallbacks` records. Normal startup creates objects 0–6 and 8, deliberately omitting object 7. The factory independently installs optional callback3, invokes on_init, and registers gobj_proc; acquisition failure reports a diagnostic and returns NULL. The shared parameter pointer is assigned before object initialization. Its structure contains thirteen integer fields and three floats—not the baseline's twelve/four split. Camera calls set near/far clipping to 1 and 1000, not camera scale. The final startup call supplies the effective lower blast-zone offset minus 50. [Source](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/grflatzone.c#L20-L186), [callback roles](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/types.h#L131-L171), [camera setters](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/cm/camera.c#L4514-L4522).

Object 1's `grFlatzone_80217430` is callback1, not gobj_proc; the separate `grFlatzone_80217438` is its empty process. Most existing structural callback names and inert-body explanations remain supported. The object-2 initializer's proposed suffix is corrected from OnLoad to OnInit to distinguish its actual role from the stage-level load callback.

### Event controller and animation selection

Objects 2 and 5 begin startup-gated. Their deferred callbacks clear xC4 after Ground invokes the stage on_start hook; Ground then frees the queue nodes. Object 2 processes events only when its gate is clear and readiness is set. Its countdown guard is `!= 0`, including negative signed-halfword states, rather than `> 0`. Entering with zero reloads a waiting interval and presentation; decrementing to zero dispatches an event. The candidate array contains only 3 and 4, activating objects 8 and 3. Switch cases 1 and 2 remain present but are not generated by that array. The initializer does not assign xC5, so the first prior selection is not established locally. [Controller](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/grflatzone.c#L203-L293), [deferred lifetime](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/ground.c#L700-L733).

Object 5 selects animations through a five-column signed-halfword table, rejecting -1 entries and repeated columns. Cycling requires a clear startup gate, row other than 8, and nonzero column. Its `xCC-- < 0` test does not expire an initial zero timer immediately. Collision refresh runs outside the cycling guards, and changed animation IDs trigger collision-joint pair maintenance. Row-selection helpers compare against xC8, but the owned code initializes that field to 8 without maintaining it as last-row history; immediate row reuse is therefore not prohibited. [Animation controller](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/grflatzone.c#L315-L386), [selection helpers](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/grflatzone.c#L696-L779).

### Moving actor and cross-file attribute lifetime

Object 3 starts hidden in phase -1. Activation selects direction and starting position, followed by waiting, periodic movement, an animation guard, conditional floor probing, a waiting phase, and hide/reset. The dynamic call registers numeric attribute 0x11 with radius 22 on an existing detected line; it does not create collision geometry. Its query applies to nearby connected floor lines, and `mpCollEnd` replaces the low byte of floor flags. Consequently, the rendered Manhole name and cover/platform explanations are not independently supported. Replacement knowledge uses the conservative object-3 actor identity, while both Manhole and Oil Panic link hypotheses remain unresolved. [Actor](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/grflatzone.c#L390-L560), [attribute implementation](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/gr/grdynamicattr.c#L30-L115), [consumer](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/mp/mpcoll.c#L793-L802).

Probe failure or exhausted attribute allocation does not prevent object 2 from being resumed at phase-2 completion. That signal precedes the actor's final hide/reset. Phase 4 clears the retained pointer without removing its record. The restart branch calls removal only when that pointer equals NULL, which the callee treats as a no-op. Attribute aging is driven separately and decrements only positive lifetimes; nonpositive lifetimes are not automatically expired by that loop. These exceptional branches and separate lifetimes are preserved rather than repaired in the explanation.

### Other objects and tools

Objects 4 and 6 initialize hidden and share a trigger relay to object 2. A trigger is consumed even when the destination object or payload is absent; collision refresh is called independently. Object 8 consumes a trigger to load its duration, spawns on countdown divisibility, randomizes horizontal position, five-way tool variant and facing, and assigns collision joint ID 0x32 only when the returned item and payload exist. A positive countdown's expiration resumes object 2; a nonpositive loaded duration does not take that completion path. Spawned Tools have independent item lifetimes.
