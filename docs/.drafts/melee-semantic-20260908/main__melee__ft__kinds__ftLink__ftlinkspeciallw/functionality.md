## Scope, identities and reconciliation
This review independently read all 139 lines of ftlinkspeciallw.c and all 16 lines of ftlinkspeciallw.h in canonical and rendered form, all 23 owned subjects, all 59 baseline facts and all 30 outgoing links. The librarian's principal corrections are confirmed: any held item blocks pull initialization; creation is deferred and allocation-dependent; the throw bit is consumed; x48 is passed as an item kind despite the local Fighter_Part declaration; collision transitions preserve the current animation frame; section-layout claims remain unproved. This review additionally leaves the baseline hand-appearance game-mapping claim unresolved rather than retaining it with a qualification that changes its meaning.

The header declares eight public void(HSD_GObj*) entry, animation, physics and collision callbacks. updateBomb and spawnBomb are static; doEnter and doAnim are additional static sharing helpers, not separately writable subjects. coll_mf is a source-level constant expression. Includes supply fighter state, common animation/physics/collision/throw/pickup operations, skeleton positioning and item construction. Padding arrays and address comments do not prove compiled layout or register allocation. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.h#L1-L16 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.c#L1-L138.

This TU implements the Link-family grounded/aerial Bomb-pull action, not the bomb item's complete explosion or destruction lifecycle. Actual Link and CLink motion-table entries install these animation, physics and collision callbacks with NULL in the interrupt slot. Link wording therefore identifies a supported consumer, not exclusivity. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlink.c#L214-L235 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCLink/ftclink.c#L222-L243.

## Entry and held-item boundary
ftLk_SpecialLw_Enter supplies 108 and ftLk_MS_SpecialLw to doEnter; ftLk_SpecialAirLw_Enter supplies 112 and ftLk_MS_SpecialAirLw. updateBomb returns false exactly when item_gobj is NULL. For every non-null item it returns true, independently of kind. Its u32 offset test accepts offsets 0 and 1 from It_Kind_Link_Bomb, corresponding to It_Kind_Link_Bomb and It_Kind_CLink_Bomb; offset 2 and ordinary below-base kinds are excluded. Accepted kinds invoke ftCo_800957F4 with the original object and selected motion ID. Other held items suppress pull setup without that call. The boolean denotes occupied slot, not successful bomb recognition. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.c#L29-L61 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/forward.h#L185-L188.

On the empty-slot path, doEnter clears throw_flags, calls Fighter_ChangeMotionState with the selected pull state, Ft_MF_None, frame 0, speed 1, blend 0 and NULL, calls ftAnim_8006EBA4, then installs spawnBomb in accessory4_cb. It does not directly invoke spawnBomb. The compatible-held-item callee instead clears command and throw state, computes animation speed, changes motion state, installs ftCo_80095EFC and immediately invokes that callback. Literal IDs 108/112 are not promoted to directional or smash-throw gameplay names without complete active table proof. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.c#L29-L61 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_ItemThrow.c#L319-L335.

## Deferred creation and ownership
spawnBomb extracts Fighter and ftLk_DatAttrs and tests ftCheckThrowB0. That inline clears throw_flags_b0 before returning true. Thus each observed event is consumed even if creation fails; no event means no construction. The callback obtains the FtPart_LThumbNb joint through ftParts_GetBoneIndex, calls lb_8000B1CC(joint,NULL,&pos), and forwards gobj, &pos, fp->ft_data->x8->x10, da->x48 and facing_dir to it_8029DD58. It stores the returned pointer, including NULL, in item_gobj; calls ftParts_80074A4C(gobj,2,1) after either constructor outcome; and invokes ftpickupitem_80094818(gobj,1) only for non-NULL. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.c#L63-L84 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/inlines.h#L245-L253.

The constructor assigns its fourth argument to SpawnItem.kind. Accordingly the local Fighter_Part part = da->x48 declaration must not be mistaken for evidence that this argument is a bone. The actual bone lookup is separate. The constructor copies the supplied vector into spawn.prev_pos, obtains spawn.pos separately via it_8026BB68, sets facing, zero velocity and fighter-parent references, and allocates through Item_80268B18. Successful initialization stores another fighter reference, initializes lifetime and forwards the third argument to Item_8026AB54. The caller's stack Vec3 pointer is not retained by this constructor. Exact visible position at creation and the semantic meaning of fp->ft_data->x8->x10 remain unresolved. Source: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itlinkbomb.c#L178-L221.

spawnBomb neither unregisters itself nor rechecks item_gobj before overwriting it. A newly asserted event can cause another attempt; failed allocation is not automatically retried after the bit has been consumed. No local destruction of an overwritten item exists. These are local facts, not proof that a conflicting overwrite occurs in normal gameplay. Fighter_8006C80C invokes accessory4_cb only under !x221F_b3 and !x2219_b5, after effect-queue flushing and transformation work. Full scheduling, interruption/death cleanup and callback reset guarantees remain unresolved. Source: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/fighter.c#L2552-L2564.

## Animation and physics
Both animation wrappers use doAnim. A false ftAnim_IsFramesRemaining result invokes ft_8008A2BC on the ground or ftCo_Fall_Enter in the air. There is no local timer or numeric frame threshold. The predicate scans eligible parts and queries primary joints when x8A4_animBlendFrames == 0, secondary joints otherwise; no qualifying active joint returns false. At source comparison level, either signed zero takes the primary branch while NaN and nonzero values take the secondary branch. This is not a cur_anim_frame > 0 test. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.c#L86-L102 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftanim.c#L515-L538.

The grounded completion helper dispatches ordinary fighters to ft_8008A348. DownSpot and another common exceptional condition can return before Wait; Link/CLink have distinct post-Wait hooks. Consequently completion uses the common neutral path rather than guaranteeing idle in every case. Source: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_08A1.c#L54-L108.

Ground physics unconditionally calls ft_80084F3C, which selects ground_friction, multiplies it by friction_when_above_walk_speed only if ABS(gr_vel) > walk_max_vel, then applies friction and ground movement. Equality does not scale friction; unordered NaN comparisons do not enter the branch under ordinary floating comparison semantics. Neither positive friction nor a multiplier greater than one is established. Air physics unconditionally calls ft_80084EEC, which applies common fall with gravity/terminal_velocity and aerial friction; it contains no local fast-fall or stick-drift selection. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.c#L104-L112 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_084E.c#L33-L53.

## Collision continuity
Ground collision changes to ftLk_MS_SpecialAirLw when !ft_80082708(gobj). Air collision changes to ftLk_MS_SpecialLw when ft_80081D0C(gobj). Each predicate receives gobj alone; the transition helper receives gobj and fp. Both transitions use coll_mf and then reinstall spawnBomb. coll_mf combines SkipMatAnim, SkipColAnim, UpdateCmd, SkipItemVis, Unk19, SkipModelPartVis, SkipModelFlags and Unk27. The common inlines perform their ground/air conversion and forward cur_anim_frame, speed 1 and blend 0 to Fighter_ChangeMotionState. This proves current-frame paired-state transfer, not every implied presentation/command preservation effect. The source retains Verify MSID TODOs. No local transition on the other branch does not prove that the collision predicate is mutation-free. Sources: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftLink/ftlinkspeciallw.c#L114-L138 and code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/inlines.h#L71-L89.

## Conventions and limits
The routines assume valid fighter objects, attributes, fighter data and skeleton indices; they supply no local null recovery for those inputs. No local finite-value validation protects facing, positions or physics attributes. Bomb initialization divides by lifetime-xC without a shown zero/finite guard, which is a dependency boundary rather than local recovery. Compiled exceptional-float behavior is not established by source inspection. No .sdata2 bytes, ordering, size or exclusive consumers, and no r3/r4 ABI allocations, are proved.

The rendered aliases ftAnim_Advance, ftCo_LightThrow_Enter, lbJObjTransformPoint, itLinkBomb_Spawn, ftParts_SetPendingModelSelection, ftpickupitem_OnItemPickup, ftCommon_ApplyFrictionGroundAndMovement, EnvironmentCollision_AllowGroundToAir and ft_CheckGround are hypotheses only. In particular the collision alias cannot override the canonical negated condition. The renderer reports no parse errors, leaves ambiguous doEnter/doAnim unchanged and correctly does not substitute the shadowed cb binding. Canonical bodies, not substituted names, support this review.
