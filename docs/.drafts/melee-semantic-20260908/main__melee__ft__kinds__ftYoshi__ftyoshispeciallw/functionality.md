## Yoshi down special
This unit implements the ground-origin, aerial, and landing phases of Yoshi's SpecialLw/Yoshi Bomb, plus a deferred paired-star accessory callback. The header declares the public move callbacks; the source additionally declares the star callback and landing-entry helper static. Proposed names are descriptive hypotheses, not recovered original symbols.

### Entry and phase progression
Ground-origin entry first calls ftCommon_8007D5D4, which explicitly sets GA_Air, clears selected movement fields, records one jump used, and installs an ECB lock of 10. Thus the SpecialLw motion name does not imply physically grounded handling. Both entry paths select their respective motion at frame 0, rate 1, zero blend, then initialize animation and reset cmd_vars[0..1], throw_flags_b0, self_vel.x/y, and translation-offset y while setting x2223_b4. These resets occur after motion-state selection, not before it. [Entry](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispeciallw.c#L44-L87), [airborne setup](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L515-L525).

Ground-origin animation exhaustion calls ftYs_SpecialS_8012EA04, which selects SpecialAirLw at frame 20 with the explicit SkipHit, SkipMatAnim, SkipColAnim, UpdateCmd, SkipItemVis, Unk19, SkipModelPartVis, SkipModelFlags, and Unk27 flags. This helper does not itself perform a ground/air conversion. Aerial animation exhaustion instead latches cmd_vars[1] without changing motion. ### Velocity and collision
Both nonlanding physics callbacks first derive self velocity from animation translation offsets through ft_80085134. Ground-origin physics clamps negative y to zero. Aerial physics assigns x114 when the derived y is numerically below x114 or cmd_vars[1] is nonzero. The source does not supply x114's numeric value or sign. [Move physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispeciallw.c#L107-L126), [shared helper](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_084E.c#L120-L125).

Ground-origin collision delegates unchanged to aerial collision. A successful ft_CheckGroundAndLedge result takes priority: cmd_vars[0] plus self_vel.y <= 0 selects landing; otherwise ftCommon_8007D5D4 resets airborne handling, contrary to the baseline's air-to-ground description. Only a failed first test reaches the cmd_vars[0]-gated cliff check and cliff-entry call. No successful guard means no explicit transition in this callback. [Collision branches](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispeciallw.c#L127-L144).

### Landing, deferred stars, and exit
Landing entry writes gr_vel = 0, invokes common grounded conversion, selects SpecialLwLanding, and installs fn_8012E644 in accessory4_cb. The common conversion can subsequently overwrite gr_vel from self_vel.x, so the initial zero write is not proof of final zero ground speed. It also handles conditional bookkeeping, resets jump-related fields, unlocks ECB, and validates ground support. [Landing entry](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispeciallw.c#L177-L185), [ground conversion](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L546-L594).

The deferred accessory callback obtains the TransN position, applies opposite horizontal star offsets and the shared vertical offset, calls it_802B2FC8 twice with directions -1 and +1, then unconditionally clears accessory4_cb. Installation and invocation are distinct events; two spawn calls do not prove successful allocation of two articles. Item ownership, lifetime, failure behavior, and callback-dispatch timing extend beyond this unit. [Star callback](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftYoshi/ftyoshispeciallw.c#L27-L42).

Landing physics delegates to ground friction and movement. Friction is multiplied above walk_max_vel, but the multiplier's magnitude is not established here. Landing animation exhaustion calls the common action-ending dispatcher: Yoshi follows the non-hand path, with exceptional guards before ordinary Wait. Landing collision literally calls Fall when ft_80082708 returns GA_Ground; that enum comparison must not be reinterpreted as a direct observation of current physical grounding.
