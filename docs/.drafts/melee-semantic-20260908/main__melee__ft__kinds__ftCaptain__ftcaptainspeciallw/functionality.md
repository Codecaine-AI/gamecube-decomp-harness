## Shared Captain-family down special

This unit implements entry, animation, physics, collision, effect-event and damage-dealt callbacks for Captain Falcon's Falcon Kick and Ganondorf's shared down special. Both character tables register this family under SpecialLw. The header declares public callbacks; the damage callback and reset/transition helpers remain local. Rendered names are hypotheses, not independent evidence. [Captain table](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptain.c#L198-L274), [Ganon table](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftGanon/ftganon.c#L198-L273).

### Entry and movement

Both entries clear command variables 0–2 and throw flags, change motion at frame 0/rate 1, perform animation setup and install effect-hitlag callbacks. Ground entry additionally initializes a counter to zero and a velocity multiplier to one, then installs the damage callback after changing state. Aerial entry does not initialize those move variables or install that callback. The damage callback increments the counter and compounds speciallw_on_hit_spd_modifier while the incoming counter is <= speciallw_unk2. The threshold is inclusive; multiplication does not guarantee slowing for arbitrary attribute values. [Entry and damage handling](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptainspeciallw.c#L85-L124).

Active grounded-entry physics selects ground preparation and ft_80085088, or neutral part-0 Z rotation and ft_80085134. It then multiplies self_vel.x/y by the stored multiplier and handles the effect event. The ground helper assigns gr_vel from animation translation when x594_b0 is set, otherwise applies friction; it is distinct from the adjacent acceleration helper. The aerial helper writes horizontal velocity from translation Z times facing and vertical velocity from translation Y. Active aerial-entry physics uses that helper and effect handling without the local multiplier. [Local physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptainspeciallw.c#L214-L304), [common physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_084E.c#L33-L125).

### Recovery and callback lifetime

Grounded-entry animation exhaustion clears commands/throws and selects SpecialLwEnd or SpecialLwEndAir according to current ground_or_air. Ground recovery passes speciallw_ground_lag_mul as animation rate; airborne recovery passes 1. These transitions reinstall effect-hitlag callbacks. Aerial-entry exhaustion clears commands/throws, calls ftCommon_8007D5D4 and enters SpecialAirLwEndAir. The common helper explicitly sets airborne mode and jumpsUsed=1, with velocity and ECB bookkeeping. [Transitions](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptainspeciallw.c#L126-L191), [air conversion](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L515-L525).

SpecialLwEnd physics retains multiplier consumption after either ground traction or airborne fall/friction. SpecialLwEndAir instead returns after its ground branch, or uses cmd_vars[0] to choose airborne fall/friction versus animation translation, without locally applying the multiplier. Aerial landing recovery selects move-specific traction with cmd_vars[2]; airborne aerial recovery uses common gravity and aerial friction. Motion-state callback installation clears deal_dmg_cb and hitlag callback slots, and ending transitions do not reinstall the damage callback. Continued multiplier consumption therefore does not imply continued hit-callback registration. [Recovery physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptainspeciallw.c#L235-L304), [callback reset](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/fighter.c#L1370-L1389).

The two grounded-move ending animation callbacks delegate to the common ground/air dispatcher. Aerial landing recovery delegates to the neutral-action helper; airborne aerial recovery delegates to Fall. These are not unconditional guarantees of ordinary Wait or Fall: the neutral path includes special-kind routing, DownSpot and another guarded alternative. [Completion callbacks](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptainspeciallw.c#L159-L212), [dispatcher](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ftcommon.c#L596-L604), [neutral exceptions](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_08A1.c#L53-L109).

### Collision and wall follow-up

Active grounded-entry collision first corrects support or landing without locally changing motion. A subsequent independent test requires cmd_vars[0] and either facing -1 with Collide_RightWallHug or facing +1 with Collide_LeftWallHug. It clears commands/throws, converts to airborne handling and enters ftCa_MS_SpecialHiThrow1. Despite its spelling, that state is registered under SpecialLw with rebound flags; SpecialHi uses Throw0. Throw1's local animation callback delegates to Fall after exhaustion and its physics uses animation translation velocity. The two contrary baseline facts are superseded and the Falcon Dive implementation link is rejected. [Wall transition](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptainspeciallw.c#L301-L333), [registrations](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptain.c#L187-L274).

Grounded-move ending collisions share logic: cmd_vars[1] selects the support predicate; failed support converts to air, while an airborne collision result can convert to ground without a local motion change. Active aerial and aerial-ending-air collision share doColl, which clears commands/throws, grounds the fighter and enters SpecialAirLwEnd at speciallw_landing_lag_mul. Preserve its literal guard ft_80081D0C(gobj) != GA_Ground. The callee returns GA_Air for a successful underlying collision result unless the exceptional ft_80081A00 branch returns GA_Ground; this return is not the resulting fighter mode. Collision checking updates positions even without a wrapper transition. [Collision suite](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCaptain/ftcaptainspeciallw.c#L335-L391), [predicate](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_081B.c#L105-L123).

### Effect protocol and evidence limits

ftCa_SpecialHi_800E3EAC consumes throw_flags_b1. Without an event it does nothing. With an event and a clear x2219_b0 latch, numeric motion_id 0x165 selects the right foot and zero angle; 0x167 selects the left foot and the configured angle multiplied by approximately pi/180. Captain spawns effect 0x490 and Ganon 0x50C. Unsupported kinds spawn nothing but still latch. Unsupported motion IDs leave local joint/angle values uninitialized before supported-kind spawn branches; their unreachability is not established. An event with the latch set invokes ftCommon_8007DB24, clearing the latch and destroying all object effects. The HandleEffect and OnHit rendered hypotheses agree with independently inspected operations and callers.
