## Input and entry
`ftCo_Dash_CheckInput` conditionally negates negative stick X, then requires magnitude >= `dash_smash_stick_threshold` and the integer-converted tilt timer < `dash_smash_window`. A negative stick/facing product calls smash-turn entry; every other qualifying product calls Dash entry with integer 1. True means an entry routine was called, not necessarily that Dash was entered. The predicate itself does not check groundedness. [Source](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Dash.c#L27-L47)

Entry clears command variable 0, selects Dash with frame/rate/blend arguments 0/1/0, performs animation setup, and assigns the tilt timer 0xFE. It computes facing-scaled initial velocity; a negative ground-velocity/facing product selects that value as the correction, otherwise existing ground velocity is subtracted. It stores the correction in dash.x0 and submits it to `ftCommon_800804A0`; this is not a direct assignment of gr_vel. The caller's integer is copied unchanged to dash.x4 and subsequently tested as zero/nonzero. A non-null x197C gates sound parameters 0x118, 0x7F, 0x40; this does not establish the pointed object's identity. [Source](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Dash.c#L49-L72)

## Animation and interrupts
Animation expiry forwards the original object to `ft_8008A2BC`. That dispatcher has Master Hand and Crazy Hand alternatives; ordinary neutral handling has exceptional checks before its normal Wait transition. This is not an unconditional Wait entry. [Dash](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Dash.c#L74-L79), [dispatcher](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_08A1.c#L54-L109).

IASA uses ordered branches, not independently active windows. With nonzero dash.x4 and frame <= x44 it checks side special, qualifying held-item LightThrowF4, catch-related input, forward smash, and the additional x48-limited check. Otherwise frame <= x4C selects side special, catch-related input, AttackDash input, opposite-facing Dash input, and a guard check receiving `(s32)(x4C-frame)`. The remaining branch checks catch-related input, Dash input, then guard and its follow-up setter. Failed paths can reach the shared fallback: `ftCo_800DE9D8`, then `fn_800CAF78`, command variable 0, and `fn_800CA5F0`.

Crucially, middle-phase `ftCo_AttackDash_CheckInput` success is not synonymous with entering AttackDash: pressed A can enter LightThrowDash, call the item-attack path for item category 2, or dispatch character-specific dash attack, including Kirby handling. Dash calls `ftCo_AttackDash_SetMv0` after every true result and then returns. The setter writes `mv.co.attackdash.x0 = p_ftCommonData->x68`. No claim is made about overlapping union bytes or effects on another state's layout. [Caller](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Dash.c#L81-L143), [callee and setter](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_AttackDash.c#L26-L82).

The footer is reached by fallthrough, including some successful transitions. Catch-related early returns, AttackDash-success postprocessing, and all paths through the false branch of `ftCo_800DE9D8` bypass it. When reached, it queries friction, computes `temp_f0 = gr_vel * x54`, then executes `gr_vel += -temp_f0 * friction`. It neither clamps nor guarantees reduction of signed velocity or magnitude. Ordinary damping of negative velocity increases its signed value; unrestricted factors can reverse or amplify velocity.

## Physics and numerical boundaries
Physics tests the numeric value dash.x0, not a dedicated initialization boolean. A nonzero value is cleared and skips steering on that invocation; zero permits steering immediately, even on the first invocation. Both branches call ground movement. Steering derives acceleration and target from stick X and character attributes, with scaled ground friction. The inline calculation does not constrain acceleration to facing, supporting the conditional moonwalking association when reversal input avoids state dispatch. [Physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Dash.c#L145-L162), [inline](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/inlines.h#L132-L140).

No local checks establish finite inputs, positive attributes, normalized facing, ordered frame thresholds, or bounded friction and x54. Under ordinary IEEE comparison semantics, NaN stick magnitude fails the threshold test; an unordered facing product fails the negative test. Signed zero is not negative and is false in the physics guard, whereas NaN is nonzero and takes the clear branch. NaN frame comparisons fail the <= guards. Infinity, overflow and invalid operations can propagate through arithmetic; no finite-output guarantee follows. The float-to-s32 remaining-frame conversion requires representability for ordinary C semantics; this source does not establish bounds or target-specific exceptional conversion behavior. Exact machine rounding and compiled instruction behavior are not asserted.

## Collision uncertainty
The local collision callback only forwards gobj to `ft_800844EC`. Its literal control flow sends a nonzero `ft_80082708` result to `ftCo_8009EDA4` and zero to Fall. However, `ft_80082708` returns `fall_off_ledge ? GA_Air : GA_Ground`, and this revision declares GA_Ground=0 and GA_Air=1. Thus the existing grounded-wall/airborne-Fall explanation cannot be retained as proven. Position synchronization is explicit, but gameplay polarity is deferred rather than silently reversing the enum interpretation. ## Ownership and lifetime
These routines borrow Fighter_GObj and its Fighter user_data; they do not allocate, free, retain or transfer ownership locally. Entry and IASA keep a Fighter pointer across calls capable of changing motion state. Correct continuation therefore depends on the object and user_data remaining valid across those calls; this TU does not independently establish that global lifetime contract. Held-item and x197C pointers are borrowed fields, not owned resources. Motion variables persist in fighter storage for later callbacks, but their Dash interpretation is state-dependent and must not be assumed to survive transitions. The post-transition footer uses the still-borrowed Fighter and whatever gr_vel the called routines leave there. No thread-safety, reentrancy, object-destruction immunity or union-layout guarantee is inferred.
