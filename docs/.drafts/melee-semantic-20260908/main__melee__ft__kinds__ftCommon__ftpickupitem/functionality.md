## Fighter-side item pickup

The single-item predicate applies eligibility/compatibility guards and strict XY overlap against fighter-relative pickup bounds expanded by item extents. The list search additionally checks requested weight bits, rejects the ftCo_800A2040 plus signed cpu.xC==28 condition, and selects strictly closer candidates below squared distance 30000. Distances are measured from each applicable region center; ties retain list order. Heavy items use the grounded-heavy offset even for airborne fighters. [Selection](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftpickupitem.c#L39-L151).

Ordinary entry requires an empty secondary slot and starts LightGet or HeavyGet without reserving the candidate. Initialization clears throw flags, changes motion, advances commands once or until B3 is observed and consumed, then installs weight-specific move state and damage handling. The loop has no local iteration limit. Animation performs a fresh search at its acquisition event. [Initialization](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftpickupitem.c#L207-L253), [consuming test](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/inlines.h#L255-L263).

Transfer assigns the primary or secondary slot, notifies character/player systems, selects an attachment part, and invokes item pickup. It does not locally protect an occupied secondary slot. Secondary-slot notification bypasses the character callback. TopN is selected for Warp Star only within the non-heavy branch; heavy attachment uses its configured part, and additional sound requires weight==1. Completion prioritizes the secondary slot. LightGet invokes immediate-effect processing; other motions with a selected item enter common or character-specific heavy holding. Generic completion is the fallback. [Transfer and completion](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftpickupitem.c#L255-L329).

Both immediate-item dispatchers require numeric classification 5 and preserve unsupported-kind no-op paths. Normal completion returns true only for Warp Star and Hammer replacement-state calls. Interruption instead releases Warp Star with a zero vector and invokes a distinct Hammer helper. Bunny Hood has no explicit local destruction call. [Normal dispatch](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftpickupitem.c#L153-L205), [interrupted dispatch](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftpickupitem.c#L343-L396).

IASA is empty, not proof that all interruption is disabled. Phys delegates ground friction and movement. Coll invokes a shared terrain check whose failure callback first attempts primary-heavy release, then processes primary and secondary slots in order, then enters Fall. Damage processes those slots without this wrapper's release/Fall calls. External item operations own destruction, release, and slot-clearing lifetimes; local code does not establish alias safety or snapshot the second slot before processing the first.
