## Old Ottosea / Topi behavior

The owned C file defines a twelve-entry ItemStateTable and the actor's initialization, damage handler, and callback groups for states 0–7. State indices are distinct from animation identifiers: states 1/2 share the UnkMotion2 callbacks with animation identifiers 0/1; states 6/7 share UnkMotion7 callbacks with identifiers 4/5. State 8 has a null collision callback. States 8–11 reference callbacks outside this translation unit. These are source-level observations, not compiled-section or layout claims. ### Initialization and ordinary movement
Initialization derives facing from position, synchronizes collision orientation, initializes x28 to 1, clears the companion pointer x20, and conditionally sets the one-shot companion request x2C. State-0 physics decrements a nonzero x28; an invocation beginning at zero enters ordinary movement. Its animation callback independently restarts state 0 when the animation predicate clears. Ordinary entry resets velocity and x24, attempts pending Freeze creation at a facing-relative offset, clears the request even on failure, and selects state 2 with a companion or state 1 without one. [Initialization](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itoldottosea.c#L47-L79) [Initial callbacks and movement entry](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itoldottosea.c#L110-L178)

Ordinary physics starts configured horizontal velocity when x24 equals 10 for JP settings or 0 otherwise, then increments x24. The animation callback tests a threshold of 42 for JP or 80 otherwise and randomly chooses slide entry or resets horizontal velocity and both counters. In the US branch, ordinary-state reselection additionally requires animation completion; the other branch reselects immediately and uses flags 0x12 for state 1. Thus x24 is not monotonic over the ordinary-state lifetime. The final floor-relative helpers run even after the guarded turn-entry call. [Ordinary callbacks](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itoldottosea.c#L180-L261)

### Terrain, falling, sliding, and turning
Ordinary collision processing may invoke turn entry for edge flags before returning its floor result. A failed floor result then selects state 4; supported movement separately checks walls. State-4 entry clears x28 and multiplies horizontal velocity by attr->x4, without establishing that the multiplier is a damping fraction. Its animation callback is a no-op; physics delegates to the common fall helper; environmental collision grounds the actor and invokes state-0 entry on landing. [Local terrain/falling group](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itoldottosea.c#L263-L304) [Edge processing](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/itgroundcoll.c#L142-L167) [Landing](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/itgroundcoll.c#L554-L581)

Slide entry resets velocity and x24 and selects state 7 with a companion or state 6 otherwise. At x24 == 25, shared physics assigns facing_dir * attr->x8; after 25 it adds -facing_dir * attr->xC. There is no explicit zero clamp, and attribute values are needed to establish relative speed. Both final floor helpers execute unconditionally, including after turn entry. Animation completion clears x28 and enters state 0. Slide collision checks walls after support processing, so a wall-triggered turn can supersede a falling transition made earlier in that invocation. [Slide group](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itoldottosea.c#L365-L435)

Turn entry stops velocity, initializes x28 to 55 for JP or 40 otherwise, and selects state 3. Animation reverses facing only on an invocation beginning with x28 == 0, then decrements the counter. Animation completion independently exits through ordinary entry; reversal is therefore not guaranteed before every exit. Turn physics is empty. ### Damage and cross-file lifetime
The damage handler saves facing, adds the helper-provided contribution to xC9C, and takes the terminal branch when the new total is strictly greater than the configured threshold OR msid == 9. Equality otherwise takes state-5 damage entry. The terminal branch handles an existing companion, performs shared notifications, requests a small camera quake, and randomly dispatches to external handlers; it still returns false. State 5 restores saved facing and applies airborne gravity without a terminal-speed clamp. Animation completion repeats state 5 in air or dispatches directional recovery otherwise. Its airborne collision helper invokes the same recovery dispatcher on floor or wall contact, so recovery is not grounded-only. [Damage handler](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itoldottosea.c#L81-L108) [Damage group](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itoldottosea.c#L306-L363) [Airborne contact callback](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/itgroundcoll.c#L646-L675)

All locally defined collision callbacks forward the shared it_802E35CC result. In itwhitebea.c, this helper sets xDCC.b3 outside blast-zone bounds and also honors an already-set flag. Its terminal path handles a retained companion, calls the shared destroyed notification, and returns true. On surviving paths, companion distance greater than attr->x18 releases and clears the association; distance at most attr->x14 updates companion velocity, using attr->x20 specifically for msid 7 and attr->x1C otherwise. The intermediate distance range performs neither operation. Mixed whitebea/freeze union-arm spellings and the stale caller comment on the clearing helper should not be treated as proof of object identity or exclusive callers.
