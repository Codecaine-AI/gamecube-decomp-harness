## Fighter-side Ice Shot lifecycle

The unit implements paired grounded and aerial neutral-special entry, animation, IASA, physics and collision callbacks, plus a shared accessory callback. The header declares the ten public state callbacks; the accessory callback has a file-local forward declaration. The actual Popo motion-state table registers the paired callbacks for states 341 and 342. [Source](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecialn.h#L1-L18), [registration](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopo.c#L20-L42).

### Entry and updates
Both entries clear throw_flags, cmd_vars[0] and x222C, enter their numeric motion state, call ftAnim_8006EBA4 and install ftPp_SpecialN_8011F500 in accessory4_cb. Aerial entry assigns attribute x4 to vertical velocity only when x224C is clear, then sets that flag and clears x2250. Otherwise it leaves vertical velocity untouched and sets x2250 to -10.0. Ground entry does not reset those two aerial fields. These are attribute-controlled mechanics, not proof of a particular upward speed. [Entries](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecialn.c#L29-L65).

Animation exhaustion calls ft_8008A2BC on ground and ftCo_Fall_Enter in air. The ground completion dispatcher normally reaches Wait, but has boss dispatch and common DownSpot/Hammer-related exceptional paths; it is not an unconditional Wait transition. Both IASA callbacks are empty. Physics delegates to shared ground friction/movement or gravity/terminal-velocity falling followed by aerial friction. Ground friction is scaled when absolute ground speed exceeds maximum walking speed. [Callbacks](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecialn.c#L67-L93), [completion](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_08A1.c#L53-L109), [physics](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_084E.c#L33-L53).

### Projectile commands and lifetime
Command 0 returns. Command 1 derives a position from joint 0, adds facing-scaled attribute xC horizontally and attribute x10 plus x2250 vertically, and calls the ice-item constructor. It stores the result in x222C and plays sound 130021 even if construction fails. Successful construction installs ftPp_Init_8011F060 in death2_cb and take_dmg_cb; command 1 is consumed regardless of success. Command 2 acts only with a non-null tracked item: it calls it_802C16F8, clears the command, selects sound 130141 for Popo or 130090 for every other kind, plays sound 130024, and performs conditional fighter-side cleanup. With no tracked item, command 2 remains pending. Other command values are also left unchanged. [Accessory callback](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecialn.c#L139-L185).

The item release routine sets horizontal velocity from item attributes, initializes release-related state and enters item motion 2. Clearing fighter tracking does not destroy the released projectile or clear its stored owner reference. Later item destruction notifies the fighter through ftPp_Init_8011F16C, whose item-identity comparison protects unrelated current tracking. The installed damage/death callback also invokes cleanup for other specials; it is not exclusively an Ice Shot callback. [Item construction/release/notification](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itclimbersice.c#L87-L156), [fighter cleanup](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopo.c#L398-L444).

### Collision and exceptional behavior
Ground collision performs cleanup only when ft_80082708 returns false, then enters Fall regardless of whether an item was tracked. Aerial collision uses the literal condition ft_80081D0C(gobj) != GA_Ground. The helper updates collision positions and returns enum labels that must not be interpreted as a direct reading of the fighter's ground_or_air field. On the guarded branch, the callback destroys a tracked item, conditionally clears tracking and callbacks, resets x224C/x2250 and calls ftCo_LandingFallSpecial_Enter(gobj, false, da->x8). That helper can instead enter HammerLanding. The source establishes attribute-driven landing lag, not a constant 16-frame duration. [Collision callbacks](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecialn.c#L95-L137), [query](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_081B.c#L105-L123), [landing exception](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Landing.c#L103-L113).

The post-item-call cleanup comparisons read live x222C fields through Fighter pointers; they do not compare against a saved pre-call item pointer. Item destruction can itself notify and modify the fighter. The aerial fields are also reset by Popo's death initialization, so the local landing reset is not their complete lifetime. [Local comparisons](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopospecialn.c#L95-L147), [death reset](code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftPopo/ftpopo.c#L383-L394).
