## Hold animation, boundaries and association
Ground and air Hold_Anim use parallel logic. frames_to_loop_charge_ground decrements whenever nonzero. frames_to_loop_charge_air decrements only while the fighter handle is NULL, also using a nonzero test. Their names therefore do not imply exclusive ground/air operation. With no handle, both updated counters <= 0 select matching End at frame zero and return; otherwise matching Hold1 is enforced, preserving cur_anim_frame. With a handle, owner mismatch clears it and returns before absent-handle processing. A matching owner and it_802AA7F0 result exactly 1 enforce Hold1. it_802AA7E4 returns Item.owner; it_802AA7F0 returns 1 exactly when Item.msid is 2, otherwise 0. No further gameplay meaning is assigned to numeric item state 2. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftKirby/ftkirbyspecialness.c#L122-L162; code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftKirby/ftkirbyspecialness.c#L188-L228; code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/it/kinds/itnesspkflash.c#L46-L60.

Negative nonzero loop counters decrement away from zero; they are not saturated. Signed minimum decrement is unguarded. A counter of 1 becomes zero before the ending test. Ownership checking assumes a dereferenceable item and does not validate dangling pointers.

## IASA and item-side consumers

Otherwise a non-NULL fighter association is cleared. Delay 1 permits release on that invocation; zero decrements to -1 then clamps. The signed-minimum boundary is unguarded. ## Physics and collision
Ground Start_Phys decrements nonzero falling_acceleration_delay then calls ft_80084F3C. Ground Hold/End_Phys only call that helper. The helper applies ground friction and movement, multiplying friction only when ABS(gr_vel) > walk_max_vel; equality does not select the multiplier. Air Start_Phys decrements a nonzero delay and skips falling on that invocation. Only a callback beginning at zero passes specialn_ns_gravity and co_attrs.terminal_velocity to ftCommon_Fall. It always calls ftCommon_ApplyFrictionAir with aerial_friction. Air Hold/End_Phys delegate to Air Start_Phys. Delay 1 therefore skips falling on the decrement-to-zero invocation; negative delays are not clamped. Repeated End_Anim resets preclude asserting an independently monotonic ending countdown without scheduler/tuning evidence. Evidence: code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftKirby/ftkirbyspecialness.c#L325-L370; code://c302741689bd67c361cd7faadb221df3193992c3/src/melee/ft/ft_084E.c#L42-L53.

Ground collision callbacks compare ft_80082708(gobj) == GA_Ground and invoke ftCommon_GroundToAirStateChange for matching Air Start/Hold0/End. Air callbacks compare ft_80081D0C(gobj) != GA_Ground and invoke ftCommon_AirToGroundStateChange for matching ground Start/Hold0/End. Preserve those exact comparisons: GA constant names alone do not establish collision-query return semantics. Helpers use cur_anim_frame, rate 1 and supplied ftCommon_GroundAirColl_MF. Hold1 may therefore convert to Hold0, affecting the item-side holding predicate.
