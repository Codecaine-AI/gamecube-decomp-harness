# Big Blue semantic sweep

## Scope and naming
The hash-bound research establishes coverage of the owned implementation, both headers, all assigned subjects and baseline links. This lead restored cached canonical/rendered evidence and independently checked the proposal and substantive contradictions. Supported inherited knowledge is retained without equivalent-wording rewrites. Rendered names remain descriptive hypotheses, not proof of original names, asset identity or compiled layout.

## Registration and lifetime
`grBb_StageData` explicitly registers Big Blue and `/GrBb.dat`. Startup caches yakumono parameters, configures stage flags, installs nine indexed Ground components, hides two joint hierarchies and invokes boundary, collision and directional-record setup. The common installer configures rendering, optional callback3, synchronous initialization and a priority-4 process; missing objects are reported, but startup directly dereferences required results used for hiding. The car-group cleanup frees and nulls its two allocations. Hiding and resetting a recurring model is not destruction. The submitted directional record's -1 count does not by itself prove indefinite lifetime.

## Three-record moving-object controller
The initializer obtains three indexed joints, resets their records, scales them and registers collision callbacks. The process coordinates activation through shared slots, initializes randomized delays, samples candidate heights and chooses entry direction. Positive delays can be cleared by other records' states and positions. With a valid edge sample, obstructed placement is retried upward and can fall back above the camera before reveal; obstruction is not an unconditional activation veto.

Active records update height and tilt from surface queries and accumulated collision loads. `grBigBlue_LaneSpeed` multiplies an indexed constant-vector component by `Ground_801C0498()`; its results affect proximity distances, rotated probes and query bounds. Horizontal translation separately uses stored `x44.x`. Directional exits hide/reset the model and return it to waiting with half a newly randomized delay. A map-collision maintenance request follows the three-record loop. Activation can also call the filtered item-spawn helper and publish shared coordination data.

## Other moving components and track
The FFlyer-diagnostic controller coordinates appearance, waiting and departure. Its raw 0xD8 field drives horizontal translation; Y is updated separately. Departure state 3 is entered before a best-effort scan for free shared capacity. The adjacent controller uses a raw map-32 +0xD0 activation dependency, height queries, timers and randomized drift; this dependency is not assigned a stronger gameplay identity.

Track setup measures twelve segment displacements, explicitly scales segment joints, initializes the current/next pair and gives road position scaled nonzero X/Y values. Segment replacement hides outgoing geometry, advances indices, selects a constrained replacement and updates collision configuration. The retry assertion uses `count++ > 1000`, not an exact 1000-attempt limit. The road controller applies transforms to its model and associated objects 1 and 2. Empty local process callbacks therefore do not imply those objects never move.

## Car-record pipeline
Thirty model slots support up to four configured records, not an unconditional four concurrently active actors. Scheduling, state selection, transition application and motion integration are separate stages. Proximity-driven packed-state insertions are conditional on MUST_MATCH; the alternative source stores unchanged loaded bytes. Numeric state meanings and raw/typed layout correspondence remain qualified.

The below-stage retirement test applies only to states 2–6 and 10. State-10 acceleration uses current X position, not side of origin. Transition state 9 can mutate state without reporting success. The aggregate updater dispatches four indexed updates and performs at most ten pair-separation passes, stopping early after a correction-free pass. The wrapper calls Ground collision maintenance after return whether or not the solver converged.

## Queries and evidence limits
Helpers provide strict proximity tests with optional secondary overrides, a layout-sensitive maximum-Y query, a highest collision-intersection query with a missing-hit sentinel, and a platform-height query whose mixed distance/Y accumulator must not be summarized as reliably nearest-height selection. Other callbacks provide a guarded fixed vector, stage-gated collision-joint classification, conditional display forwarding, a NULL descriptor and a strict origin-height comparison.

The final proposal corrects bounded termination, controller data flow and placement fallback. Compiled sections, jump tables, ABI offsets, pointer rebasing, register bindings, exact visible actor/item/sound identities, PR provenance and unverified downstream consumers remain deferred. The origin helper may perform matrix/cache work, so the shadow comparison is not described as transitively mutation-free.
